# Seed Match Shortlist

This shortlist identifies candidate matches for initial Mystery Player seed puzzles under the current rules: both starting XIs, all 22 starters hidden, one global guess input, no substitute guessing.

Accessed date for web sources: 2026-05-08.

## Assumptions

- Shortlist entries are not finished seed data. Each match still needs full starter-by-starter verification before publication.
- WorldFootball.net is treated as a reputable secondary database for initial lineup research, not as the only required publication source.
- Official organizer pages are preferred when they list teams or useful context. Some official articles are retrospective and should be paired with a direct lineup database before seed authoring.
- Candidate difficulty is judged for a general football-history audience.

## Strongest First Seed Candidates

1. **2022 FIFA World Cup Final: Argentina vs France**: best mix of official FIFA teams, modern recognition, and full 22-player fairness.
2. **UEFA EURO 2020 Final: Italy vs England**: official UEFA lineups, high familiarity, strong audience hook.
3. **2005 UEFA Champions League Final: Liverpool vs Milan**: iconic match, strong name recognition, excellent puzzle context.
4. **2012 Premier League: Manchester City vs QPR**: unforgettable ending and a useful difficulty spread, though QPR hints need care.
5. **2010 FIFA World Cup Final: Netherlands vs Spain**: globally meaningful with many recognizable starters, moderate difficulty.

## Candidate Matches

### 1. Argentina vs France

- **Date:** 2022-12-18
- **Competition:** FIFA World Cup Final
- **Why it matters:** Lionel Messi's first World Cup win, Kylian Mbappe hat-trick, and one of the most dramatic finals in tournament history.
- **Source links:**
  - [FIFA Greatest Games: Argentina v France](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/articles/argentina-france-2022-final-greatest-games)
  - [WorldFootball.net match report](https://www.worldfootball.net/report/wm-2022-in-katar-finale-argentinien-frankreich/)
- **Likely difficulty:** Easy
- **Puzzle suitability:** Excellent. Both XIs have many modern, globally recognizable players and FIFA directly lists the teams. Alias work needed for accents and Spanish/French name forms.

### 2. Italy vs England

- **Date:** 2021-07-11
- **Competition:** UEFA EURO 2020 Final
- **Why it matters:** England's first men's major final since 1966 and Italy's first EURO title since 1968, decided on penalties at Wembley.
- **Source links:**
  - [UEFA final line-ups article](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0269-1262855338b0-a7a22fe4ebb4-1000--euro-2020-final-italy-vs-england-line-ups-all-the-finalists-sta/)

- **Likely difficulty:** Easy
- **Puzzle suitability:** Excellent. Official UEFA source lists the final starting XIs and the player pool is familiar to current audiences. Some Italian accent and surname aliases should be checked.

### 3. Liverpool vs Milan
